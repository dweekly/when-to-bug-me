# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Stage 6c: the decision as a conversation, on the page.** Below the card, the person and Bug Me take turns: words or a click go to `/api/converse`, Bug Me's reply appears with the firefly, and the person's turns are marked with what it heard. Once Bug Me reads the decision back, the primary button is the decision itself; pressing it sends it down the usual path (a typed "yes" lights the same button): pushback, the revealed guess and guidance, which continue in the thread. Other courses and rejected questions are recorded as such and can still become guidance; open questions go to the agent; nothing decided opens Skip. "I'm curious: why?" stays quiet when the conversation already asked. The thread is kept with the answer and re-opens read-only; a decision being talked through is not re-routed under the person. The as-you-type parse and per-question asks are gone from the page. Tours talk through each decision with Jordan's scripted turns. Saved mornings from earlier versions start fresh.
- **Stage 7: live fire.** Real Claude Code sessions can ask Bug Me through a small MCP server (`mcp/bugme.mjs`, tools `ask_bug_me` and `check_bug_me`). Bug Me routes each ask against the owner's rules (starting from eleven distilled from David's CLAUDE.md), settles only what a permission-granting rule covers within $1, and queues the rest on a private page, `/me`: the same app, signed in with a one-time link. The owner's answer, reason and guidance go back to the asking session. A per-owner Durable Object holds the queue; nothing from it reaches the repo, logs, cache or recordings. Off unless `LIVE_TOKEN` is set.
- **Stage 6d: Decided for you, redesigned.** One sentence says what the jobs are, once. They are grouped by kind of work (merged branches, patch updates, formatting and lint, flaky tests), each group closed until opened, with one short line per job: what was done, in the past tense (from the fast model's titles), and the project. Every job has the same three icon actions, Undo, Ask! and Disagree, quiet until hovered or focused and always labeled on phones. A group's rule shows only when asked. No route glyphs, rule codes or per-row Why.
- **Stage 6b: the conversation, server side.** New `/api/converse`: one turn of the decision conversation, with Claude's reading of the thread (choice, why, conditions, questions with answers from the agent's material, feedback on the question, "just do it") checked quote by quote and put through `advance`, which caps Bug Me at two questions, forces a read-back before anything closes, and writes that read-back itself when it overrides the model (prompt 2026-10-12.2, eval 8/8 conversations, 24/24 trials). `/api/fact` takes outcomes outside the options (another course, or the question rejected). Each decision gets an emoji, a short title and past-tense "done" lines from Claude Haiku 5.5, recorded for 39 of 40. The tours' conversations are scripted and recorded. Settled housekeeping fixtures carry a hand-authored kind of work. The page doesn't use any of this yet (stage 6c).
- **Stage 6a: fixes from David's staging review.** "Not now" is "Skip"; the way forward sits at the bottom right of every action row, with Skip or Cancel at the far left. The microphone is a button inside the box speech fills; the corner firefly only shows the glow. Home suggests a project ("Suggested first: Launch project", "Begin Launch") and pluralizes its tallies. "More context" comes before the question. Hovering an option no longer looks like picking it. Tours offer Jordan's recorded words on live servers too, where they replay for free.
- **Stage 5: a morning that matches the proposal.** 40 questions overnight: Bug Me settles 31 housekeeping calls itself (formatting, lint fixes, patch-level bumps, merged branches, flaky-test quarantines, each cleared by rule R2 or R7) and 9 wait for Jordan, Launch first. Fifteen consequential questions that could not honestly be settled left the morning; the three with pictures stay as test fixtures. Saved mornings from earlier versions start fresh.
- **Stage 4: think out loud.** One place on the card to talk a decision through; Bug Me marks the choice, reasons, conditions and questions in the person's own words as they go (new `/api/parse`, prompt 2026-10-12.1, eval 18/18). The Why panel, "Yes, but…", "Something else", "Ask a question" and the answer Undo window are gone: talking covers them. Reasons and conditions go into the record and guidance verbatim.
